# NVIDIA Corporation — Form 10-K, Fiscal Year 2023

Item 1. Business

NVIDIA Corporation designs accelerated computing platforms and GPUs. This annual report covers fiscal year 2023.

Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis (MD&A)

Revenue moved from $16,675 million in fiscal 2021 to $26,974 million in fiscal 2023, a
compound annual growth rate of +27.2% CAGR over the period. Management
discusses the drivers of the trend and the outlook for the coming fiscal year in
this section.

Key risks include competition, supply-chain and component concentration, and
macroeconomic and foreign-exchange volatility across the company's markets.

Item 8. Financial Statements

Consolidated Statements of Operations (in millions, fiscal year 2023).
For fiscal year 2023, total revenue was $26,974 million. Gross profit was
$15,356 million. Operating income reached $4,224 million. Net
income for the year totaled $4,368 million.

Consolidated Balance Sheet (in millions, fiscal year 2023).
Total assets stood at $41,182 million. Total current assets were
$23,073 million. Total current liabilities were $6,563 million.
Total debt was $10,953 million. Total shareholders' equity was
$22,101 million. Cash and cash equivalents were $3,389 million.

Research and development expense was $7,339 million for the fiscal year.
