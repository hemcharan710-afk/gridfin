# Microsoft Corporation — Form 10-K, Fiscal Year 2023

Item 1. Business

Microsoft Corporation develops and licenses software, cloud services and devices. This annual report covers fiscal year 2023.

Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis (MD&A)

Revenue moved from $168,088 million in fiscal 2021 to $211,915 million in fiscal 2023, a
compound annual growth rate of +12.3% CAGR over the period. Management
discusses the drivers of the trend and the outlook for the coming fiscal year in
this section.

Key risks include competition, supply-chain and component concentration, and
macroeconomic and foreign-exchange volatility across the company's markets.

Item 8. Financial Statements

Consolidated Statements of Operations (in millions, fiscal year 2023).
For fiscal year 2023, total revenue was $211,915 million. Gross profit was
$146,052 million. Operating income reached $88,523 million. Net
income for the year totaled $72,361 million.

Consolidated Balance Sheet (in millions, fiscal year 2023).
Total assets stood at $411,976 million. Total current assets were
$184,257 million. Total current liabilities were $104,149 million.
Total debt was $47,237 million. Total shareholders' equity was
$206,223 million. Cash and cash equivalents were $34,704 million.

Research and development expense was $27,195 million for the fiscal year.
