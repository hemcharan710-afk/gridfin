# Apple Inc. — Form 10-K, Fiscal Year 2023

Item 1. Business

Apple Inc. designs and sells consumer electronics, software and services. This annual report covers fiscal year 2023.

Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis (MD&A)

Revenue moved from $365,817 million in fiscal 2021 to $383,285 million in fiscal 2023, a
compound annual growth rate of +2.4% CAGR over the period. Management
discusses the drivers of the trend and the outlook for the coming fiscal year in
this section.

Key risks include competition, supply-chain and component concentration, and
macroeconomic and foreign-exchange volatility across the company's markets.

Item 8. Financial Statements

Consolidated Statements of Operations (in millions, fiscal year 2023).
For fiscal year 2023, total revenue was $383,285 million. Gross profit was
$169,148 million. Operating income reached $114,301 million. Net
income for the year totaled $96,995 million.

Consolidated Balance Sheet (in millions, fiscal year 2023).
Total assets stood at $352,583 million. Total current assets were
$143,566 million. Total current liabilities were $145,308 million.
Total debt was $105,103 million. Total shareholders' equity was
$62,146 million. Cash and cash equivalents were $29,965 million.

Research and development expense was $29,915 million for the fiscal year.
